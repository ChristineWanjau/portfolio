# Portfolio

Christine Wanjau's one-page portfolio for recruiters, framed as an art gallery: software engineering presented as art.

## Language

**Gallery**:
The whole portfolio site: a single screen with a plain, minimal wall, where work is hung as framed pieces. Van Gogh appears only inside the frames.
_Avoid_: website, landing page

**Piece**:
One framed item on the gallery wall. In v1, each Piece is a role from Christine's experience (not a side project).
_Avoid_: card, tile

**Room**:
The detail view a visitor enters by clicking a Piece: one large framed painting with the full Placard notes, plus tabs to move to the other Rooms and a way back to the Gallery wall.
_Avoid_: modal, detail page

**Placard**:
The museum-style label on a Piece: title, medium (tech stack), years, and a one-line story.
_Avoid_: caption, description

**Medium**:
The technologies a Piece was made with, written like an art medium ("React & TypeScript on Azure").
_Avoid_: tech stack (in UI copy)

**Artist statement**:
The hero headline and short intro that says who Christine is and what she builds.
_Avoid_: bio, hero text

**Signature**:
Contact links (email, GitHub, LinkedIn) and the CV download, placed like an artist's signature in the corner.
_Avoid_: footer, contact section
